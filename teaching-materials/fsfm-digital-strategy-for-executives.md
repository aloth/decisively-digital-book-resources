## 2. Case Study Analysis: JudgeGPT

This assignment requires you to interact with JudgeGPT, a research project focused on critically evaluating news content generated by AI. The goal is to explore the nuanced perceptions of authenticity in machine-generated vs. human-generated news and gather insights on how individuals discern between real and artificial news content.

1.  **Read the Research:** To understand the academic background of the project, please review the paper: [Blessing or curse? A survey on the Impact of Generative AI on Fake News](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03021).
2.  **Perform the Analysis:** Use the web-based interface at [https://judgegpt.streamlit.app/](https://judgegpt.streamlit.app/) to evaluate the news fragments.
3.  **Submit Your Findings:** Submit your analysis and observations via the Google Form at [https://forms.gle/EUdbkEtZpEuPbVVz5](https://forms.gle/EUdbkEtZpEuPbVVz5).
